Our Story

From Dev Shop to Product Company in 7 Years.

Founded in 2019 as a bespoke software development company, Wenthura has grown into a team of 35+ permanent engineering professionals with deep product, technology, and domain expertise — building purpose-built platforms and running enterprise services. Supported by an on-demand network of freelance specialists and trusted delivery partners, we bring the right expertise to every engagement.

Headquartered in Colombo, Sri Lanka, we build software platforms, engineering teams, and education technology solutions for businesses across the region. Our solutions support organisations in Sri Lanka, UAE, Thailand, and Cambodia.

2019
Founded as a bespoke software development company with custom builds for early clients.
2020–2023
Delivered 30+ custom solutions across multiple industries. Grew the team to 35+ permanent engineering professionals.
2024
Launched Nena AI — AI-powered learning platform for students and institutions.
2025–2026
Launched DoodleNest & AutoFlow. Expanding through a growing network of regional partners.
